# Deungbul Saju — Privacy Policy (English draft)

공개 전 초안이다. 한국어 방침(`privacy-policy.md`)과 내용이 같아야 하며, 다를 때는 한국어본이 우선한다.

Operator: Martin park · Contact: knkbpark@gmail.com

1. What the app stores. Your birth date, birth time, birthplace, sex, name and personality type, and your match partner's details, are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers; we do not operate one.
2. Face photos. A photo you take or choose for face reading is analyzed on your device only. Only the positions of facial landmarks are measured. The photo and the measurements are not saved and are not sent anywhere. No one is identified from a face.
3. Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Purchase status is confirmed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase records. We never receive your card details.
4. Advertising. Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data under Google's privacy policy. The app does not show the tracking permission prompt on iOS and does not use the advertising identifier there.
5. Deleting your data. "Erase all saved data" at the bottom of the screen removes everything stored on the device. Deleting the app also removes it.
6. Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.
7. Changes. Changes to this policy are announced in the app or on this page.
